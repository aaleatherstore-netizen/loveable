FAQ

Common Sawalaat

Aap ke sawalon ke jawab.

Kya sach mein 3 din mein store ready ho jata hai?

Ji haan. Starter package 3 din mein deliver hota hai. Professional 5–7 din, Enterprise 10–14 din.

Payment kab leni hoti hai?

50% advance shuru karte waqt, baki 50% delivery se pehle.

Domain aur hosting ka kharcha alag hai?

Shopify ki monthly subscription (around $29/month) alag hai. Domain hum free connect kar dete hain (.com aap ka).

Kya products bhi aap add karte hain?

Ji haan — Professional & Enterprise packages mein 50+ products tak hum add karte hain with descriptions & images.

Pakistan ke liye payment gateways setup karte hain?

Bilkul. JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer, COD — sab configure karte hain.

Meta Pixel aur ads setup hota hai?

Ji — Professional & Enterprise mein Pixel, Conversions API, aur GA4 fully setup kar ke dete hain.

Agar launch ke baad changes karwane hon?

Har package mein free support period hai (7/15/60 din). Uske baad affordable rates per maintenance available hai.
