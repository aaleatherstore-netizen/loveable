FAQ

Common Sawalaat

Jo aksar loog puchhtey hain — seedhe jawab.

Kya main Shopify ke baare mein kuch jaane baghair store banwa sakta hun? +
Bilkul! Aapko kuch jaanne ki zaroorat nahi. Hum shuru se aakhir tak sambhaltey hain aur delivery ke baad training bhi dete hain taake aap asaani se apna store manage kar sakein.
Payment kaisy karni hai — safe hai? +
Payment bilkul safe hai. Hum 50% advance lete hain — baaki 50% sirf tab jab aap store dekh kar khush ho jaayein. Bank transfer ya Easypaisa/JazzCash — jo aapko suit kare.
Kya store delivery ke baad koi changes karwaa sakta hun? +
Haan! Delivery ke baad hum free revisions dete hain. Agar koi cheez aapko pasand nahi toh hum change karengy — bina kisi extra charge ke.
Shopify ki monthly fee kaun dega? +
Shopify ka Basic plan $29/month hai jo aap khud Shopify ko directly pay karte hain. Hum sirf store banane ka charge lete hain — koi hidden fee nahi. Starter package mein hum pehle mahine ka trial bhi setup karte hain.
Pakistan mein Shopify se online payment ho sakti hai? +
Haan! Hum aapke store mein multiple payment methods setup karte hain — Cash on Delivery (most popular), bank transfer, aur Stripe bhi (if eligible). Aapka koi sale nahi rukeyga.
Meta Ads ke liye alag package lena hoga? +
Starter package mein Meta basic setup (Page, Business Portfolio, Pixel) included hai. Professional aur Enterprise mein full campaign management bhi included hai. Ads ka actual budget aap khud Meta ko dete hain — woh hamare charges mein nahi ata.
Kitne din mein results dekhne ko milenge? +
Store 3 din mein ready ho jaata hai. Ads chalane ke baad pehle 7 din mein data aana shuru hota hai aur 2-4 hafte mein consistent results dekhne lagein ge — yeh aapke product, budget aur niche pe depend karta hai.
Kya aap sirf store banate hain ya full business setup bhi karte hain? +
Sirf store nahi — hum aapka poora digital presence setup karte hain. Store + Meta Business + Pixel + Ads strategy + training — sab kuch ek jagah. Aapko alag alag logon ke paas jaane ki zaroorat nahi.
